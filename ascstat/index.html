Impressions are how often the app appeared in search or browse. From there a listing can be opened or installed directly — the Get button sits in the results list, so most Get taps never involve a product page view. Page views and Get taps are therefore both measured against impressions, not against each other. Downloads counts first-time installs, re-downloads and restores together, since a Get tap produces whichever applies.
