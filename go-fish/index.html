Go Fish


The Game

This is a classic fun card game suitable for even the youngest card players.

In our version you play against AI.

The cards are dealt from the standard 52-card deck, and each player gets seven cards to start.

The goal is to win the most “books” of cards. A book is any four of a kind, such as four kings, four aces, and so on.

The player asks the opponent to give them all cards of a specific rank, for example" “Give me your nines”. In our game you do it by tapping one of your cards. If the opponent has any of such cards, they must give all of them to the player who asked. Otherwise they say “Go fish!” and the player must take a card from the deck, by tapping it.

As long as the player is getting the cards they asked for, either from the opponent or from the deck, their turn continues. If the player does not receive the card they asked for, the turn passes to another player.

Menu

Any time during the game tap the menu symbol at the top-right.

  • New Game - start a new game.

  • Settings - bring up the Settings page.

  • Scoreboard - see the number of times you have won and lost the game. You can get to the Game Center Leaderboard to compare your score to other players.

  • How To Play - shows documentation pages.

  • About - show information about the app.

Settings

You can configure different options the way you like it:

  • AI Level - select Easy, Medium or Hard level.

  • Card size - select Small, Medium or Large.

  • Separate cards by rank - spread cards of different ranks horizontally.

  • Stagger cards by rank - shift cards of different ranks vertically.

  • Auto-fish for player - deal the card when the player is sent to Go fish.

Score

If a player completes a “book” (four cards of the same rank), they are placed on the table in front of that player, face up. If at any time a player is left with no cards, they get one card from the deck and the game continues.

The game ends when all the books are collected.

The player with the most books is the winner.
